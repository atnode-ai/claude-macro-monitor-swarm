# Cross-indicator chain (strategist reasoning spec)

Sequence the latest prints along their lead/lag relationships, then judge alignment.

- Financial conditions (curve slope, HY spreads, NFCI) -> real economy, lead about 6 to 18 months. Market-implied Fed path and 5y5y breakevens price the policy and inflation outlook in real time: the leading edge of the chain.
- ISM Prices Paid -> PPI (1 to 3 months) -> CPI -> PCE -> Fed path.
- Initial claims -> JOLTS / ADP -> payrolls (labour turn). AHE -> CPI.
- ISM New Orders / retail sales / durable goods -> GDP.
- Permits -> starts -> residential GDP.
- Conference Board labour differential -> unemployment rate (1 to 3 months). UMich long-run expectations -> Fed reaction function.
- FOMC / ECB / BoE decisions are the cumulation of all of the above.

A single surprise is noise. **Several domains telling the same story is signal.** Example: ISM Services below 50 + rising claims + falling JOLTS + soft retail sales = labour market turning; if CPI is still hot at the same time, that is the dual-mandate dilemma. Name these alignments explicitly, and say what would confirm or deny a regime change.
