# Housing lens (starts, permits, new and existing home sales)

- Permits lead starts by about a month; both track mortgage rates and feed residential investment in GDP.
- New home sales are contract signings (leading, about 10 to 15% of sales); existing home sales are closings (lagging, 85 to 90%).
- Strong prints support homebuilders, lumber and REITs and signal household balance-sheet strength; weak prints flag affordability stress and a GDP drag.
- Units: starts, permits and new home sales are thousands SAAR; existing home sales millions SAAR.
