# Growth & activity lens (GDP, retail sales, ISM, IP, durable goods, trade, CARTS)

- ISM New Orders lead GDP; ISM Prices Paid lead PPI/CPI; ISM Employment leads payrolls. Above 50 is expansion.
- Retail sales (control group) feed GDP consumption. Core capex orders (nondefense ex-aircraft) proxy business investment.
- GDP summarises nearly everything else; advance, second and third estimates share a quarter, so only the first print is "new".
- CARTS (Chicago Fed) is a nowcast of Census retail sales ex-autos: log it as a preview, no market consensus, alert only on divergence from the hard print.
- ISM is not on a free API: fetch the ISM Report On Business page (or two reputable news write-ups if the page blocks you) and record PMI, New Orders, Prices Paid, Employment.
