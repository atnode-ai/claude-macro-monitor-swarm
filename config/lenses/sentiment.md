# Sentiment & surveys lens (UMich, Conference Board, Ifo)

- UMich 5 to 10 year inflation expectations are a Fed anchoring signal; a rise is a hawkish catalyst. The collector reads the sentiment index from the survey's own table: the mid-month preliminary is logged with `vintage: prelim`, and the final reading replaces it later in the month (the preliminary figures stay under `preliminary`). Treat a preliminary-to-final revision as the same release, not a new trend.
- Conference Board: the labour differential (jobs plentiful minus hard to get) leads the unemployment rate by 1 to 3 months.
- Ifo proxies German and euro-area momentum, which feeds EUR and European equities.
- Soft sentiment tends to foreshadow softer retail sales, but sentiment and spending decouple often; don't over-weight a single survey.
