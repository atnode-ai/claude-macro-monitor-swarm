# Labour lens (NFP, claims, JOLTS, ADP, UK labour market)

- Initial claims are the highest-frequency labour signal and lead payrolls. JOLTS quits lead wage growth. Average hourly earnings feed CPI. ADP is an imperfect NFP proxy; don't over-read a divergence.
- A **labour-market turn** is rising claims + falling JOLTS openings + soft payrolls together. Call it out explicitly when two of the three line up.
- Strong jobs with low inflation is equity-bullish; strong jobs with hot wages is not.
- UK: claimant count, unemployment rate and regular pay growth (AWE) drive the BoE path.
- Consensus: payrolls change, unemployment rate and AHE m/m from the main poll; claims consensus is the weekly survey figure.
