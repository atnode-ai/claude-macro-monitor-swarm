# Central banks & policy lens (FOMC, ECB, BoE, euro-area HICP and GDP)

- Policy decisions are the cumulation of all other data. Hawkish (higher rates or dots, firmer guidance) means USD up, yields up, equities vulnerable, gold lower; dovish is the reverse.
- The rate level comes from the collector (FRED target range, ECB deposit rate, BoE Bank Rate). You add what no API carries: vote split, guidance tone, SEP/dot-plot median (quarterly Fed meetings), from the official statement page.
- A hold is still a decision: record it with tone. Minutes and speeches matter only when language shifts.
- Euro-area HICP drives the ECB path and EUR; FRED carries the final print, Eurostat publishes the flash earlier.
