# Financial conditions & markets lens (curve, spreads, NFCI, SLOOS, breakevens, Fed path)

- You own market-priced data only. Real-activity nowcasts belong to Growth; policy decisions belong to Central Banks. Classify by what a series measures, never by publisher (the Chicago Fed publishes both NFCI, yours, and CARTS, Growth's).
- Financial conditions lead the real economy by about 6 to 18 months. Curve inversion leads recession; dis-inversion from inverted often precedes the downturn itself.
- Material: curve regime change or a 20bp+ weekly move; HY OAS 25bp+; NFCI crossing zero; 2y yield 18bp+ (then check CME FedWatch once for the implied path); 5y5y breakeven 15bp+; every SLOOS release.
- Multi-signal alignment is the tell: curve dis-inverting + HY widening + NFCI tightening together means conditions are turning restrictive ahead of the real economy.
- VIX and the broad dollar are corroboration, not standalone signals.
